A beachfront stay on Maniwaya Island, Marinduque, with clear water, palm-lined grounds and evenings to linger.

Maniwaya Island, Santa Cruz, Marinduque

Island days. Evenings to linger.

Islas Moriones sits on the west coast of Maniwaya Island. Spend the day on the sand or in the pool, then return to an air-conditioned room with breakfast for two included.

The Beach

Shoreline Days

The resort opens onto a sandy beach on Maniwaya's west coast, with outrigger boats moored offshore, a volleyball net on the sand and a bamboo raft on the water. All are free for staying guests.

Accommodations

Rooms to Return To

Choose from standard rooms for one or two, loft rooms with a balcony and sea view, the Pool Suite or the thatched Luxury Kubo for groups. Every room is air-conditioned, with a private bathroom and hot shower.

Getting Here

Find Your Way

Islas Moriones is on Maniwaya Island in Santa Cruz, Marinduque. Most guests travel from Metro Manila to General Luna Port in Quezon, then cross to Maniwaya by public boat or one of the resort's private boats. Boat trips depend on the weather and sea conditions.
