The resort at a glance

Everything in one island getaway

A beachfront resort on Maniwaya Island, with places to stay, eat, gather and unwind within a few steps of each other.

  • 16 air-conditioned rooms
  • Outdoor swimming pool
  • Spacious camping grounds
  • Restaurant and bar
  • The Genesis Events Place and conference facilities
  • Recreational spaces
  • Wi-Fi and 24-hour electricity
