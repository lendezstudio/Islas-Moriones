Explore Maniwaya

There's more beyond the shore.

Make Islas Moriones part of your Maniwaya visit, with time to enjoy the resort and explore the surrounding islands.

  • Nearby · Offshore from Maniwaya

    Palad Sandbar

    A natural stretch of sand with no permanent structures. It fully appears at low tide, when the surrounding water is shallow and calm. Plan your visit around the tide.

  • Nearby · Mompong Island

    Ungab Rock Formation

    A natural arch formation on neighboring Mompong Island. Ask about current excursion options and arrangements before setting out.

  • Nearby · Maniwaya's south-east coast

    Sunong Bato

    A rock formation on the coast with wide views over the sea.

  • Nearby · North of the resort, west coast

    Morion Reef

    A snorkelling area along Maniwaya's west coast, with an underwater Morion statue among the coral.

Island hopping and other excursions are separate from room rates. Island visits and boat trips depend on the tide, the weather and the arrangements available for your dates, and itineraries can change on the day.

Ask About Getting Here

Practical questions

A few details before your island stay.

Can't find what you need? Ask a Travel Question.

Where is Islas Moriones?

The resort is on Maniwaya Island in Santa Cruz, Marinduque, on the island's west coast. Contact us for the exact arrival location and current travel arrangements.

How do I ask about rooms and rates?

Send your preferred dates and the number of adults and children in your group. Ask about suitable rooms, current rates and what is included. The rates on this page are the resort's published rates, effective 15 February 2026.

Can I visit with family or friends?

Yes. Rooms range from options for one or two guests to the Pool Suite's Family Suite, ideal for 8–10. Ask about the room options and arrangements for your group size. Availability and room suitability should be confirmed before booking.

What's included with a room?

Every room is air-conditioned and has a private toilet and bath with a hot shower. Complimentary breakfast for two guests is included, along with free use of the swimming pool, ping-pong, darts, volleyball, badminton, bonfire and bamboo raft.

Are children or extra guests charged?

There's no extra charge for children aged 7 and below. Guests beyond a room's ideal capacity are ₱600 per person, per night.

Can I visit for the day?

Day visits run from 8 AM to 5 PM at ₱350, subject to availability. They include communal toilets and showers and use of the swimming pool, volleyball, bamboo raft, badminton, tables and chairs.

Is there backup power?

The resort has a 75 kVA backup diesel generator.

Are boat transfers included?

Boat transfers aren't listed in the published room rates. Ask the resort about the departure point, boat timing and any transfer arrangements for your dates. Crossings depend on weather and sea conditions. Plan your journey.
