01

Welcome

Let the island set the pace.

Spend time by the beach, take a dip in the pool, and return to the comfort of your room. At Islas Moriones, your island stay can be as unhurried as you make it.

Come with someone special, bring the family, gather your friends, or bring your team. Pets are welcome too.

Every room
Air-conditioned, with a private toilet and bath with hot shower
Breakfast
Complimentary breakfast for two guests
Free to use
Pool, ping-pong, darts, volleyball, badminton, bonfire and the bamboo raft
Power
75 kVA backup diesel generator

What's new

Skylight Dining and a pickleball court, currently in progress. Ask the resort about either when you book.
