Activities

Maniwaya island hopping

Choose three destinations.

Pick any three stops around Maniwaya and neighboring Mompong Island. Island hopping needs a minimum of 6 people. Eco and barangay fees in Brgy. Maniwaya, Brgy. Mompong and General Luna town are paid separately. Trips depend on the tide and the weather on the day.

Ask About Island Hopping

  • Palad Sandbar

    A natural sandbar that fully appears at low tide, in shallow, calm water.

  • Sunong Bato & Cliff Tree

    A stacked-rock formation and a lone clifftop tree on Maniwaya's south-east coast.

  • Morion Reef Raft

    A floating raft over the Morion Reef snorkelling area, north of the resort.

  • Ungab Rock Formation

    Limestone arches on neighboring Mompong Island.

Getting Here

Islas Moriones is on the west coast of Maniwaya Island, Santa Cruz, Marinduque. From Metro Manila, travel to General Luna Port in Quezon, then cross by boat.

  1. 1

    Bus from Cubao

    Take Superlines or Daet Express from the Cubao terminal. Look for buses signed Aurora / Mulanay / Unisan. The 11:30 PM departure is recommended.

    4–6 hoursAir-conditioned bus ₱530Ordinary bus ₱450one way, per person

  2. 2

    Tricycle to General Luna Port

    Get off at the General Luna crossing in Quezon and ride a tricycle to the port.

    About 10 minutes₱30–50

  3. 3

    Public boat to Maniwaya

    One public boat a day leaves General Luna Port for Maniwaya Port.

    Departs 9:00–9:30 AMAbout 45 minutes₱250 per person, one way

  4. 4

    Walk to the resort

    Islas Moriones is a 5–10 minute walk from Maniwaya Island Port.

  1. 1

    Drive to General Luna Port

    Set Waze to General Luna Port, Quezon Province. It's about 230 km via the Pan-Philippine Highway.

    4–6 hours

  2. 2

    Through the Bondoc Peninsula

    The route passes the Bondoc Peninsula. Make sure you take the right road, and watch for falling rocks.

  3. 3

    Park at the port

    At General Luna Port, look for Jun Damian, the Islas Moriones coordinator: 0916 470 3341.

    Parking ₱100 / day

  4. 4

    Cross to Maniwaya

    Take the public boat (once a day at 9:30 AM, ₱250 per person), or book a private boat that can go straight to the resort at any time of day. Life vests must be worn.

    45–60 minutes

  5. 5

    Arrive

    From Maniwaya Island Pier, a free boat shuttle takes you to Islas Moriones.

Public transport fares and boat times are from the resort's travel guides and may change. Boat trips depend on the weather and sea conditions.

Good to Know

Where is Islas Moriones?

On the west coast of Maniwaya Island in Santa Cruz, Marinduque. The island is reached by boat, most often from General Luna Port in Quezon. See how to get here.

How do I get there from Manila?

Take a bus from Cubao or drive about 4–6 hours to General Luna Port, then take the public boat (once a day, in the morning) or one of the resort's private boats. The resort also rents vans for groups.

How do I check rates and book?

See current rates and book direct on the resort's online booking page. You can also find Islas Moriones on Booking.com, or message the resort on Facebook.

What's included with a room?

Every room is air-conditioned with a private toilet and bath with hot shower. Breakfast for two guests is included, along with free use of the pool, ping-pong, darts, volleyball, badminton, bonfire and bamboo raft.

Are children charged?

Children aged 7 and below stay free. An extra charge applies for guests beyond a room's ideal capacity.

Is there backup power?

Yes. The resort has a 75 kVA backup diesel generator.
