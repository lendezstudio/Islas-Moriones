Your Maniwaya Getaway

Let the island set the pace.

Spend the morning by the sea, head out exploring, or stay close to the pool. At Islas Moriones, there’s room for an easy escape for two, a family holiday, or a gathering with friends.

Island Experiences

Follow the water.

Make a day of exploring beyond the resort. Discover Palad Sandbar, Sunong Bato, Ungab Rock Formation, and Morion Reef Raft through island-hopping arrangements, or explore Maniwaya by kayak, bicycle, or e-cart.

Eat & Drink

Gather around good food.

Start with breakfast at Easter Sunday Café, return for Filipino favourites and seafood, or bring everyone together for a beachside boodle fight. Shared meals are part of the island experience.
