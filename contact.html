Good to know

Before you arrive

Pet policy and more →
Where is Islas Moriones?

On the west coast of Maniwaya Island in Santa Cruz, Marinduque. The island is reached by boat, most often from General Luna Port in Quezon. See how to get here.

How do I get there from Manila?

Travel to General Luna Port in Quezon by bus or by car, then cross to Maniwaya by boat. The resort can help with private boats and van rental for groups. See the travel guide.

How do I check availability and book?

Check availability and book direct on the resort's online booking page. You can also find Islas Moriones on Booking.com, or message the resort on Facebook.

What's included with a room?

Every room is air-conditioned with a private toilet and bath with hot shower. Breakfast for two guests is included, along with free use of the pool, ping-pong, darts, volleyball, badminton, bonfire and bamboo raft.

Are children charged?

Children aged 7 and below stay free. An extra charge applies for guests beyond a room's ideal capacity.

Is there electricity and Wi-Fi?

Yes. The resort lists 24-hour electricity, supported by a 75 kVA backup diesel generator, and Wi-Fi.
