Getting Here

Plan the journey. Enjoy the arrival.

Your trip to Maniwaya includes a boat crossing. Use the resort’s travel guides to explore your route, then confirm your transport and arrival arrangements before departure.

Route 1

By public transport

  1. Take a bus from the Superlines or Daet Express terminal in Cubao, Quezon City. The resort’s guide lists buses signed Aurora, Mulanay or Unisan.
  2. Get off at the General Luna crossing in Quezon and take a tricycle to General Luna Port.
  3. Cross to Maniwaya Port by public boat. Check the current departure time before you travel.
  4. Confirm with the resort how you will get from Maniwaya Port to Islas Moriones.

Route 2

By private vehicle

  1. Drive from Metro Manila to General Luna Port, Quezon Province via the Pan-Philippine Highway (about 230 km in the resort’s guide). The route passes the Bondoc Peninsula.
  2. Park at the port (a parking fee applies) and meet the resort’s port coordinator. Ask the resort for contact details before you set out.
  3. Cross by public boat or by one of the resort’s private boats. Life vests must be worn.

Boat crossings are subject to weather and sea conditions. Confirm current schedules and arrangements with the resort before travelling.

Private boat arrangements

Cross on your own schedule.

The resort’s private boats make round trips with island hopping, from General Luna Port in Quezon or from Alobo/Buyabod Port on mainland Marinduque. Message the resort for rates and availability.

  • M/V Rogelius

    Small boat · up to 6 people

  • M/V St. Longinus

    Medium boat · up to 16 people

  • M/V Omon Express

    Big boat · up to 25 people

Van arrangements for groups

Travel together.

The resort offers round trips from Manila to General Luna and back in Foton high-roof vans seating 12 to 14 passengers, with the driver, fuel, tolls and parking included.
