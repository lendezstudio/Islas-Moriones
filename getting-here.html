Getting Here

Your journey to Maniwaya

Islas Moriones Beach Resort is on Maniwaya Island, Santa Cruz, Marinduque. Plan your route and confirm arrival arrangements with the resort before setting out.

Where to find us

On the west coast of Maniwaya

Maniwaya is an island off Santa Cruz, Marinduque, reached by boat. Most guests from Metro Manila travel to General Luna Port in Quezon, then cross to Maniwaya.

Planning your crossing

Two ways to reach the port

From the resort’s own travel guides. Confirm current schedules and arrangements before travelling.

By public transport

  1. Take a bus from the Superlines or Daet Express terminal in Cubao, Quezon City. The resort’s guide lists buses signed Aurora, Mulanay or Unisan.
  2. Get off at the General Luna crossing in Quezon and take a tricycle to General Luna Port.
  3. Cross to Maniwaya Port by public boat. Check the current departure time before you travel.
  4. Confirm with the resort how you will get from Maniwaya Port to Islas Moriones.

By private vehicle

  1. Drive from Metro Manila to General Luna Port, Quezon Province, via the Pan-Philippine Highway. The route passes the Bondoc Peninsula.
  2. Park at the port (a parking fee applies) and meet the resort’s port coordinator. Ask the resort for contact details before you set out.
  3. Cross by public boat or by one of the resort’s private boats. Life vests must be worn.

Private boat arrangements

The resort’s boats can make round trips from General Luna Port in Quezon or from Alobo/Buyabod Port on mainland Marinduque. Message the resort for availability.

Van arrangements for groups
