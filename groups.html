Teams & companies

Where teams grow beyond the workplace

Bring your team together for team-building activities, leadership seminars, company outings and corporate retreats in a beachfront setting, with conference facilities, event spaces, island dining and island hopping all in one place.

Packages can be customised around your group size, preferred activities, accommodation, meals, length of stay and budget.
