Experiences

Make the most of island days

Spend time enjoying the resort, head onto the water, or ask about experiences further afield. Explore what is included with your stay and what can be arranged separately.

1 · Included with your stay

Time to enjoy, already part of your stay

Free for staying guests, from the resort’s own amenities list.

Use of the bonfire area is included; bonfire menu packages are arranged separately. A pickleball court is under development.

2 · Arranged with the resort

More ways to enjoy the island

Head onto the water or explore Maniwaya with the resort’s rentals. Additional charges apply; ask the team about availability and arrangements for your dates.

Crystal-clear kayaks

See the water from a different perspective in a crystal-clear kayak. Ask the resort about availability and rental arrangements. A rowing guide can be arranged.

Boat rentals

Plan time on the water with the resort's boat rental options. Contact the team to discuss the available boats and arrangements for your visit.

M/V Rogelius

Small boat for up to 6 people.

M/V St. Longinus

Medium boat for up to 16 people.

M/V Omon Express

Big boat for up to 25 people.

Boat trips depend on the weather and sea conditions. Boat rental is arranged separately from island-hopping packages and arrival transfers.

Mountain bikes

Ride Maniwaya at your own pace. A tour guide can be arranged.

Land tour by e-cart

See more of Maniwaya on a land tour by electric cart.

Snorkelling gear

Masks, snorkels and fins are available. Photo for illustration.

Island hopping

See what lies beyond the shore

Choose three destinations from the resort’s island-hopping options. A minimum of six guests applies, and separate ecological and barangay fees may apply. Routes and stops depend on tides, weather, and sea conditions.

Offshore

Palad Sandbar

A natural sandbar that fully appears at low tide.

South-east Maniwaya

Sunong Bato

A stacked-rock formation above clear water.

Mompong Island

Ungab Rock Formation

Limestone arches around a sheltered cove.

Off the west coast

Morion Reef Raft

A floating raft above the Morion Reef snorkelling area.

Other stops in the resort’s brochure include the Century Cliff Tree and the Morion Statues.

3 · Useful extras

Little things that help

  • Laundry service
  • In-room massage
  • Karaoke
  • Tour guide
  • Kitchen and griller use

Additional charges apply. Ask the resort for current rates.

Kayaks, boats and island hopping are arranged with the resort. Check availability for your stay, then message the team.
