Experiences

A little adventure. Plenty to enjoy.

Head out on the water, explore the island, or spend the day enjoying the resort. There’s more than one way to make Maniwaya your own.

Island Hopping

See what lies beyond the shore.

Choose three destinations from the resort’s island-hopping options, including Palad Sandbar, Sunong Bato, Ungab Rock Formation, and Morion Reef Raft.

A minimum of six guests applies. Separate ecological and barangay fees may apply. Routes and stops depend on tides, weather, and sea conditions.

Message the Resort

  • Palad Sandbar

    A natural sandbar that fully appears at low tide, in shallow, calm water.

  • Sunong Bato & Cliff Tree

    A stacked-rock formation and a lone clifftop tree on Maniwaya's south-east coast.

  • Morion Reef Raft

    A floating raft over the Morion Reef snorkelling area, north of the resort.

  • Ungab Rock Formation

    Limestone arches on neighboring Mompong Island.
