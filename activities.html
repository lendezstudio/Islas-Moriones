Activities

Maniwaya island hopping

Choose three destinations.

Pick any three stops around Maniwaya and neighboring Mompong Island. Island hopping needs a minimum of 6 people. Eco and barangay fees in Brgy. Maniwaya, Brgy. Mompong and General Luna town are paid separately. Trips depend on the tide and the weather on the day.

Ask About Island Hopping

  • Palad Sandbar

    A natural sandbar that fully appears at low tide, in shallow, calm water.

  • Sunong Bato & Cliff Tree

    A stacked-rock formation and a lone clifftop tree on Maniwaya's south-east coast.

  • Morion Reef Raft

    A floating raft over the Morion Reef snorkelling area, north of the resort.

  • Ungab Rock Formation

    Limestone arches on neighboring Mompong Island.
