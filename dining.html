Dining

Easter Sunday Café

Where island breeze meets flavorful feasts.

The resort's own café serves Filipino and American breakfasts in the morning, then soups, grilled dishes, seafood and pasta through the afternoon and evening. Skylight Dining is the newest place to eat at the resort.

Breakfast
7:00 – 9:00 AM
Lunch & dinner
11:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Easter Sunday Café menu

The Morning Feast · 7:00 – 9:00 AM

Filipino Breakfast

Beef tapa, tocino, corned beef, longganisa or dried fish · sunny side up or scrambled egg · plain or garlic fried rice · brewed coffee, hot chocolate, tea or juice

American Breakfast

Toasted bread or waffles · Spam, corned beef or pork & beans · sunny side up or scrambled egg · brewed coffee, hot chocolate, tea or juice

Continental Breakfast

Toasted bread or grilled cheese panini, with butter & jam · sunny side up egg or vegetable omelette · brewed coffee, hot chocolate, tea or juice

Junior Favorites

Hotdog, nuggets or bacon, with French fries or toasted bread · scrambled egg or cheese omelette · hot chocolate or juice

Outdoor food packages

Camp Bonfire Barbecue Grill

Grill your own by the fire: pork barbecue, hotdogs, Pinoy street food or s'mores, and a drink.

Boodle Fight at the Beach

Soup of the day, steamed crabs or shrimps, grilled squid, meat and fish, eggplant salad, rice and fruit, shared on banana leaves. It can also be set up on the bamboo raft.

Prefer to cook? Kitchen use, griller use and paluto (the café cooks what you bring) can be arranged. Corkage applies to outside food and drinks.
