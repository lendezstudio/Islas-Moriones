Campsite

Stay closer to the outdoors.

Choose a tent accommodation package or bring your own tent. Explore the campsite facilities and ask the resort about arrangements for your dates.

  • Waterproof big tent with mattress, pillows and linens, or pitch your own
  • Communal toilets and showers, and a charging station
  • Use of the pool, bonfire, volleyball, bamboo raft and badminton
