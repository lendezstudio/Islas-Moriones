Day tours, events & group stays

Campsite

Stay in a waterproof big tent with mattress, pillows and linens, or bring your own tent. Communal toilets and showers, a charging station, and use of the pool, bonfire, volleyball, bamboo raft and badminton.

Day tours

Visit for the day, 8 AM to 5 PM, subject to availability. Use of the pool, volleyball, bamboo raft, badminton, tables and chairs, with communal toilets and showers.

Events at The Genesis

An events place for 100–120 guests, air-conditioned or not, for celebrations and corporate gatherings. Up to 5 hours, with extra hours and setup by arrangement.

Exclusive stays & group packages

Planning a reunion, retreat or company trip? Ask the resort about exclusive use and packages for your group.
